03

Logo Usage

The Deadreckoner mark must be used consistently. Follow these rules to maintain visual integrity across all touchpoints.

DO. Maintain clear space.
◻ ◻ D ◻ ◻

Maintain minimum clear space around the logomark equal to the height of the D-mark's counter space.

DON'T. Stretch or distort.
D

Do not alter the logo's proportions, rotate, skew, or apply any other distortion.

DO. Use approved colourways.
D D D

The mark may appear in Primary Teal, White, or Muted Grey. No other colours.

DON'T. Add effects.
D

Do not add shadows, outlines, glows, gradients, or any other visual effects to the logomark.

06

Voice & Tone

Deadreckoner speaks with technical authority and Scandinavian restraint. Clear, direct, never decorative.

DO. Be direct and specific.

"6 assets pending screening" — clear, quantified, actionable. The user knows exactly what requires their attention.

DON'T. Use filler or jargon.

"We wanted to let you know that some items may need your review soon" — vague, passive, wastes attention.
