05 / 08

Voice & Tone

How the brand sounds

Direct, plain-spoken, and specific. No inflated claims, no filler.

Do

"Your files stay in your own Drive. We never copy them."

Don't

"Revolutionary cloud-native synergy for your digital assets."

Do

Lead with the concrete fact, then the benefit.

Don't

Lead with an adjective and back into the fact.

07 / 08

Iconography

Icon style

Thin stroke (1.25–1.5px), no fills, consistent 20×20 grid. Icons never carry brand color as a background fill — only as the stroke itself when emphasis is needed.
